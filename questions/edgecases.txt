This is an adventure-mystery that focuses on the complex relationship between Sherlock Holmes and his nemesis, James Moriarty. I love these characters and love pastiches in general - this book is a massive exception. I won’t even be placing this on my Holmes Bookshelf. This book suffers from throwing too many elements into the mix - mastermind criminal, plot to overthrow the kingdom, cryptid arachnids with magical venom, a cabal of powerful men drawn to a remote location for mind-altering experiments, dramatic navel-gazing about a dark secret death that is never fully elucidated... I mean, damn, it’s only 300 pages! The author suffers from Little Boy Story-Telling - there is SO much action and SO much evil and SO much overwrought emotion and SO much glossing over of the “boring” parts of the story that actually make it a cohesive whole - and it is distractingly tangential to read.
