QUESTION: We surveyed 100 surgeons: Name the most common indications for diagnostic EGD
1. Upper GI bleeding (30)
2. Dysphagia (25)
3. GERD/reflux symptoms (20)
4. Abdominal pain/dyspepsia (15)
5. Suspected malignancy (10)

QUESTION: We surveyed 100 surgeons: Name the most important steps in safe laparoscopic entry
1. Verify insufflation pressure (25)
2. Insert trocar at 90-degree angle (20)
3. Confirm intraperitoneal placement (20)
4. Check for injury before proceeding (15)
5. Ensure adequate pneumoperitoneum (10)
6. Use appropriate trocar size (10)

QUESTION: We surveyed 100 surgeons: What are the most common complications of colonoscopy?
1. Perforation (40)
2. Bleeding (30)
3. Cardiopulmonary events (15)
4. Infection (10)
5. Missed lesions (5)

QUESTION: We surveyed 100 surgeons: Name the essential elements of the laparoscopic timeout
1. Patient identity/procedure (25)
2. Trocar placement plan (20)
3. Equipment check (20)
4. Patient positioning (15)
5. Anticipated complications (12)
6. Conversion threshold (8)

QUESTION: We surveyed 100 surgeons: What are the contraindications to EGD?
1. Suspected perforation (30)
2. Severe cardiopulmonary instability (25)
3. Recent MI/unstable angina (20)
4. Uncooperative patient (15)
5. Severe coagulopathy (10)
